# Aeolus licensing

Aeolus is licensed under the **GNU Affero General Public License version 3**, using the SPDX identifier `AGPL-3.0-only`, unless a file or component explicitly states otherwise. The full licence text is in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE).

## Why AGPL

Aeolus is intended to remain genuinely open source while ensuring that improvements to the covered platform do not disappear into a closed hosted fork. The AGPL permits use, modification, redistribution and commercial use under its terms, and includes an additional source-availability obligation when a modified covered program is made available for users to interact with over a network.

## Third-party software

Aeolus depends on third-party packages and software with their own licences. Those components remain governed by their respective licence terms. The Aeolus licence does not replace or override third-party licences.

## Connectors, SDKs and ecosystem packages

The project may publish some future connector SDKs, connectors, examples or other ecosystem packages under a separate permissive licence where that makes integration easier. A component uses different terms only when its own licence file or package metadata says so.

## Alternative commercial terms

The copyright holder may choose to offer Aeolus under separate commercial terms for organisations that need rights different from the AGPL. No alternative licence is granted merely by this statement; any such licence would require a separate written agreement.

## Contributions

Aeolus wants to remain open to outside contributions while preserving the option of separate commercial licensing in the future. Before substantial external code contributions are merged, the project intends to establish contributor terms that give the maintainer the rights needed to do both. Until those terms are published, contributors should open an issue before starting substantial code work. See [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Brand

The software licence governs the code. It does not by itself grant rights to present a modified project or third-party product as the official Aeolus project. A separate trademark or brand policy may be published in the future.
